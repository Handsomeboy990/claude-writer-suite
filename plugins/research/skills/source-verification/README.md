# source-verification

The skeptic's pass: traces a claim to its origin, confirms the source says what
it is quoted as saying, tests the origin's authority, independence, method and
recency, cross-checks against independent sources, and catches the circular
citation where many sources trace to one unverified origin.

- Inputs: a claim and the sources that carry it, from source-research.
- Outputs: verification verdict, provenance trace, contradiction notes, confidence rating.
- Depends on: research-core.
- Downstream: synthesis-reporting, competitive-analysis, the business and career skills.

A claim repeated by a hundred sources can trace to one that made it up. Three
sources citing one origin are one source. An unverified claim is reported as
unverified, never laundered into a fact because it is convenient.
