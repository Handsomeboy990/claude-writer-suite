# synthesis-reporting

The output half of research: states the conclusion first, supports it with
attributed evidence, distinguishes fact from inference from opinion, quantifies
confidence per conclusion, surfaces disagreements and gaps, and recommends
without overreaching what the evidence supports.

- Inputs: attributed findings and verdicts from source-research and source-verification.
- Outputs: research answer, evidence trail, confidence statement, open questions.
- Depends on: research-core, source-research.
- Downstream: report-writing, the career and business skills.

Research that is never written up clearly was not done, as far as the reader is
concerned. A report that withholds the conclusion until the end forces every
reader to redo the synthesis. Confidence is stated per conclusion, not as one
blanket hedge.
