# Assessing hackathon fit

A hackathon fits a participant only relative to their skills, eligibility,
availability, and goal. Assess against all four; a strong match on one and a fail
on another is not a match.

## The hard filters (fail = out, regardless of appeal)
- [ ] Eligibility: can the participant actually enter and win? (region, status,
      age, team size) Read the actual rules, not the event name.
- [ ] Availability: is the participant free for the real event window and can they
      commit the hours?
- [ ] Format: online, in-person, or hybrid, and does that match what the
      participant needs?
- [ ] Team: does the required team size match how the participant wants to enter?

## The fit assessment (for events that pass the filters)
- [ ] Skills: can the participant build something competitive in this theme with
      what they know?
- [ ] Interest: does the theme motivate them enough to sustain a weekend build?
- [ ] Goal alignment: does the event serve their reason for entering?

## Goal changes the fit
```
to win           events with a real prize AND a strong skills/theme match;
                 eligibility to win verified (not just to compete)
to learn         beginner-friendly events, mentorship, forgiving themes; a prize
                 is secondary
to network       events with the right community and collaboration, in-person or
                 strong online community
portfolio        events whose output is a shippable, demonstrable project,
                 regardless of prize or placement
```

## Reporting the fit
- Strong: passes all filters, strong on skills, interest, and goal.
- Worth considering: passes filters, good on two of the three fit axes.
- Stretch: passes filters but weak on skills or interest; labelled as a stretch,
  not oversold.
- Not a match: fails a hard filter; said plainly, with the rule that fails.

## The effort principle
One well-chosen hackathon entered fully beats three entered half-heartedly. The
shortlist names the few worth the participant's real weekend, ranked by fit and
goal, and says where the others fall short.
