# opportunity-core

Constitution of the opportunity tree. The shared discover-then-evaluate method
behind ideation, hackathons and business research; the rule that an opportunity
is grounded in reality and never fabricated; the evaluation discipline that ranks
before it recommends; and the refusal to mistake a long list for a good answer.

- Inputs: the actor (person or team) and the field to explore.
- Outputs: opportunity method, grounding standard, evaluation discipline, shortlist.
- Depends on: nothing. Copy the directory and use it alone.
- Downstream: every opportunity skill refers to it and none restates it.

A long list is the easy half and the useless half. The deliverable is always the
ranked few with their reasoning and next steps. An opportunity is grounded in
something verifiable or marked a hypothesis; a fabricated one costs real time
chasing something that is not there.
