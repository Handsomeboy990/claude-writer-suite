# lead-research

Deepens a qualified prospect into an approach: researches the organisation and
the specific person from live sources, finds the real current reason to reach out,
identifies the right contact and channel, and drafts an outreach angle grounded in
evidence rather than a template.

- Inputs: a qualified prospect and its signal from client-discovery.
- Outputs: prospect dossier, outreach angle, contact path, timing assessment.
- Depends on: opportunity-core.
- Downstream: the outreach message, cover-letter, a messaging connector on confirmation.

The difference between a personalised message a busy person reads and a template
they delete. Every fact is sourced; outreach is prepared, not sent without
confirmation. A message built on a wrong or invented detail is worse than a
generic one, because it signals carelessness to the person you want to impress.
