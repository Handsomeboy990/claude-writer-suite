# market-research

Researches a market to inform a business decision from verifiable evidence: sizes
the market bottom-up rather than with a top-down fantasy, maps the real
competitors and substitutes, finds the actual segments and their needs, identifies
trends and gaps, and states confidence and sources throughout.

- Inputs: the market or segment to assess, the actor, the decision.
- Outputs: market sizing, competitor map, segment analysis, opportunity assessment.
- Depends on: opportunity-core.
- Downstream: idea-evaluation, client-discovery, synthesis-reporting.

A business decision built on an invented number fails expensively. "We only need
1 percent of a huge market" is a warning sign, not a plan. Every figure is sourced
or marked an estimate with its method. Reporting a small or crowded market
honestly is the most valuable output this can produce.
