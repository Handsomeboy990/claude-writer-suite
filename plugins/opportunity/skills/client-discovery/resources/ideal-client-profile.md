# Building the ideal client profile

The profile decides who is worth approaching. Built from the provider's real
offering, it excludes as much as it includes, and it centres on the signal of
need that separates a prospect from a name.

## The components

```
offering        what the provider genuinely does well, and for whom; the profile
                follows the real capability, never an aspiration to serve everyone

fit criteria    the observable characteristics of an organisation that needs and
                can pay for this: type, size, sector, stage, situation, the
                technology they use

signal of need  the observable sign that the need is real and current:
                - a public complaint the offering addresses
                - a hiring pattern implying the pain (an unfilled relevant role)
                - a growth event that outstrips what they have (a funding round, a
                  new market, a product launch)
                - a public incident or problem the offering solves
                - a technology choice that the offering serves

reachability    is there a real path to a real decision-maker or channel

exclusions      who does NOT fit, stated explicitly, so effort is not wasted
```

## The signal is the point

A list of organisations that merely fit the criteria, with no signal, converts at
the base rate of cold outreach, which is poor. The signal makes outreach timely
and welcome: approaching a company the week after a funding round about scaling
their backend is a different conversation from a cold pitch.

Rank prospects by fit times reachability times timing. A strong fit with a current
signal and a reachable contact is the top of the list. A strong fit with no signal
is a name to watch, not a prospect to approach now.

## The grounding rule
Every organisation, contact, and signal on the shortlist is from a live source.
Never a fabricated company, contact name, email, or signal. When a source cannot
be reached, hand the provider the exact directories and searches to run, rather
than inventing prospects, because they will spend real outreach effort on the list.
