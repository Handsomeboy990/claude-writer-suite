# interview-preparation

Prepares a candidate for a real interview: builds answers from true experience in
a recallable structure, drills the behavioural and technical questions the role
will actually ask, rehearses the gap and the hard question honestly, prepares the
candidate's own questions, and runs a realistic mock with follow-ups.

- Inputs: the profile, the role, company-research.
- Outputs: answer bank, question drills, gap responses, candidate questions.
- Depends on: career-core, career-profile.
- Downstream: a post-interview reflection updates the profile.

An interview tests whether the CV's claims survive a conversation. A fabricated
story collapses under a follow-up, and interviewers ask follow-ups. The most
valuable preparation is the fluent, honest answer to the gap the role exposes.
