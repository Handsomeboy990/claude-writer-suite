# company-research

Researches a prospective employer before a candidate applies or interviews: what
the company does and how it earns, its health from real dated signals, its
culture from balanced sources, the role's real context, and the red flags a
candidate should weigh. Every fact is sourced live, never invented.

- Inputs: the target company and the role, the reachable live sources.
- Outputs: company profile, health signals, culture assessment, interview intelligence.
- Depends on: career-core.
- Downstream: cover-letter, interview-preparation, job-search.

A candidate joins a company, not a job posting. An invented fact about an
employer is worse than none, because the candidate makes a real decision on it.
Signals are presented with their source and date, not as predictions.
