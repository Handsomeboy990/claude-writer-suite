# Example: turning duties into quantified achievements

Six CV lines from a real profile, rewritten from duty to achievement. Every
number comes from the candidate's actual history; none is invented.

## Before and after

```
before  Responsible for the company's deployment process
after   Rebuilt the deployment pipeline, cutting release time from 40 minutes to
        6 and enabling daily deploys where the team had shipped weekly

before  Worked on improving system reliability
after   Reduced production incidents by a third over two quarters by introducing
        automated rollback and health-gated releases

before  Mentored junior developers
after   Mentored four engineers, two of whom were promoted to mid-level within
        a year

before  Involved in API design
after   Authored the API standards RFC now used by all six backend teams

before  Handled the database migration
after   Led the zero-downtime migration of the primary Postgres cluster, moving
        <scale> rows with no customer-facing outage

before  Part of the on-call rotation
after   As on-call lead, cut mean time to recovery from 90 to 35 minutes by
        rewriting the incident runbooks and alert thresholds
```

## What changed, and what did not

```
changed   each line now names the result, quantified, with an accurate verb
unchanged the facts: every number and outcome is from the profile's real history
formula   action verb + what was done + quantified true result
```

## The line with no number

The candidate also "organised the team's onboarding docs", which produced no
measurable result they can cite. Rather than invent a figure, the honest version
is qualitative and still concrete:

```
after   Reorganised the team onboarding documentation, cited by two new hires as
        the reason their first-week ramp was faster
```

A real qualitative outcome beats an invented percentage, and it survives the
interview question "how do you know it helped?"

## The lesson

None of the rewrites added to the truth. They surfaced the results that were
already in the work and stated them in the CV's only strong unit: the quantified
achievement. The parser sees the keywords; the human sees the impact; the
interview finds every claim supportable.
