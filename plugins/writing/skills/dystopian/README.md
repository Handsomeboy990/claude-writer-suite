# dystopian

Credible dystopia: the law that the system works, four levers of control
including the intimate, the language of power, a lucid character who pays for
their lucidity, possible endings, and a ban on the stated thesis.

- Inputs: the system, its real benefit, the population.
- Outputs: documented control system, outline.
- Depends on: `writing-constitution`, `science-fiction`, `world-builder`.
- Neighbours: `political-fiction`, `cyberpunk`.

## When to use

Totalitarian regimes, surveillance, any controlled society.

## The order of writing

What the system solves, first. What it costs, second. A dystopia whose system
delivers nothing has nobody to convince, and its critique lands on nothing.
Section 8 requires at least one sympathetic character who sincerely believes
in it.

## Configuration

`language.creative_output` sets the output language.
