# haiku

Haiku in French: flexible count, local season marker, the cutting technique
and the gap between images, prohibitions (aphorism, metaphor, stated feeling),
composition in series.

- Inputs: a concrete perception, a season, a place.
- Outputs: haiku, seasonal series.
- Depends on: `writing-constitution`, `poet`.

## When to use

For a short form, a notation, or a seasonal collection.

## The element most often missed

The cut. Not the syllable count. A haiku with a correct count and no gap
between its two images is a sentence broken into three lines, and section 7
scores it accordingly.

## Local seasons

Section 5 matters outside Japan and outside temperate Europe. A haiku written
in the tropics has dry and rainy seasons, not four, and the marker must be a
verifiable local phenomenon rather than an imported almanac.
