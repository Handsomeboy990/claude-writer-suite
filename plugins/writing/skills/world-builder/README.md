# world-builder

Coherent world construction: material constraint, geography, economy, power,
belief, technology, language, special systems, revelation through use.

- Inputs: genre, research dossier, dramatic needs.
- Outputs: world bible, internal rules, cartography, lexicon.
- Depends on: `writing-constitution`, `research-director`.
- Lateral: `immersion-director`, `timeline-manager`.

## When to use

To create a world, check its consistency in cascade, or define the rules of a
magic or technology system.

## When not to use

For a real place or period. Those are documented by `research-director`, not
invented, and section 8 of the constitution applies in full.

## The two rules that do the work

The rule of the tenth: the reader sees a tenth of what you build; the rest
exists to keep that tenth consistent. And the seven cascade checks of section
4, which expose ninety percent of inconsistencies on their own.

## Configuration

`language.creative_output` sets the output language.
