# cyberpunk

Materialist cyberpunk: the augmented body and its maintenance debt, the
economy of information, competing powers, the informal economy, dense prose,
critique through detail.

- Inputs: novum, city, power relations.
- Outputs: cyberpunk bible, power map.
- Depends on: `writing-constitution`, `science-fiction`.
- Neighbours: `dystopian`, `thriller`.

## When to use

Urban near-future fiction, hackers, corporations, anywhere the subject is what
technology does to a body that cannot afford it.

## What keeps it from being set dressing

Every augmentation carries a recurring maintenance cost. That debt is the
social engine of the genre, and section 7 requires it to be named rather than
implied. Neon and rain are not the genre; the price of a meal is.

## Configuration

`language.creative_output` sets the output language.
