# espionage

Espionage fiction: the trade researched at level 3, tension of exposure,
surveillance scenes, the loyalty tree, betrayal coherent in retrospect, an
ambiguous ending.

- Inputs: the operation, the services, research dossier.
- Outputs: operation dossier, loyalty tree.
- Depends on: `writing-constitution`, `thriller`, `research-director`.
- Neighbours: `political-fiction`, `detective`.

## When to use

Narratives of agents, sources and intelligence services, where the subject is
loyalty and its cost.

## The structure that makes betrayal work

The loyalty tree. Every character serves several entities, and betrayal comes
from the ranking among them rather than from a change of character. The test
in section 4: on a reread, the traitor was coherent from the first page.

## Configuration

`language.creative_output` sets the output language.
