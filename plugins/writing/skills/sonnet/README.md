# sonnet

French, Italian and English sonnet: rhyme arrangements, placement of the
volta, stanza economy, technical constraints, composition starting from the
turning line.

- Inputs: a core, a sonnet type.
- Outputs: sonnets.
- Depends on: `writing-constitution`, `poet`.

## When to use

To write or correct a sonnet, or any fixed fourteen-line form.

## The thing to get right

Not the syllable count, the turn. Section 5 starts composition from line 9 or
line 14, whichever carries the volta, and works outward. A sonnet written
front to back arrives at its turn by accident.

## Output language

Prosody and rhyme rules follow `poet` section 2, which is French. Another
output language replaces them with its own.
