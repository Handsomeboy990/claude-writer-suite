# historical-fiction

Historical fiction: the rule of the present, reconstructing mentalities, level
3 research requirements, handling language, real historical figures, the
author's note.

- Inputs: period, place, research dossier.
- Outputs: historical dossier, outline, author's note.
- Depends on: `writing-constitution`, `research-director`,
  `immersion-director`.
- Neighbours: `political-fiction`, `adventure`.

## When to use

Any narrative set in the past, and any time an anachronism hunt is needed.

## The rule everything follows from

Characters do not know what is coming. They never announce that they live in a
pivotal age. Great events reach them through the price of bread. Section 9
raises the threshold to 4 on documentary accuracy and mentalities, because
those are the two the reader checks.

## Configuration

`language.creative_output` sets the output language.
