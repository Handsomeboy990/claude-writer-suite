# business

Finding clients, reaching them, and knowing whether the market is real. Three
skills, governed by [../ideation/opportunity-core](../ideation/opportunity-core/).

| Skill | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [client-discovery](client-discovery/) | define the ideal client profile, find real signalled prospects, qualify them |
| [lead-research](lead-research/) | deepen a prospect into a sourced, grounded, honest outreach draft |
| [market-research](market-research/) | size a market bottom-up, map real competitors, assess honestly |

Every prospect, contact, competitor, and market figure is real and sourced, or
marked an estimate with its method. A fabricated prospect wastes real outreach; an
invented market figure fails a real budget. Outreach is prepared, never sent
without confirmation.
