# client-discovery

Finds real prospective clients for a service or product from live sources, never
invented: defines the ideal client profile from what the provider actually offers,
searches for organisations that fit and show a real signal of need, qualifies each
on fit and reachability, and reports a shortlist with the evidence and a real
point of contact.

- Inputs: the provider's real offering and the reachable live sources.
- Outputs: ideal client profile, qualified prospects, fit evidence, outreach shortlist.
- Depends on: opportunity-core.
- Downstream: lead-research, market-research, the outreach message.

The difference between a name and a prospect is the signal of need. A profile that
fits everyone converts at random. Every prospect is a real organisation from a
live source with a real contact; a fabricated one wastes real outreach.
