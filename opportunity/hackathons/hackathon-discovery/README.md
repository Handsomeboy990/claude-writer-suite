# hackathon-discovery

Finds real hackathons that match a person or team from live sources, never
invented: builds the search from skills, eligibility, location, availability and
interests, queries reachable listings, and reports each with its real link,
dates, eligibility rules, prizes and theme, plus an honest fit assessment.

- Inputs: the participant's skills, eligibility, availability, interests, goals.
- Outputs: search profile, matched hackathons, eligibility check, entry shortlist.
- Depends on: opportunity-core.
- Downstream: hackathon-strategy, ideation-engine, pitch-and-demo.

A hackathon is a real event with a real deadline and real eligibility rules. An
invented one, or a real one with a misremembered deadline, sends someone to build
for nothing. Eligibility and both deadlines are verified against the source, not
taken from the event's name.
