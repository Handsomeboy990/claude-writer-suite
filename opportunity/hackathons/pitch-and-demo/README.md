# pitch-and-demo

Builds the pitch and demo a hackathon, demo day, or investor meeting is judged
on: a story that leads with the problem and stakes, a demo that shows the working
thing rather than describing it, a structure that fits the time limit, honest
claims, and a rehearsed delivery that survives nerves and a failing wifi.

- Inputs: the built project and the judging criteria from hackathon-strategy.
- Outputs: pitch narrative, demo script, slide outline, delivery plan.
- Depends on: opportunity-core.
- Downstream: adapts to demo day, investor pitch, internal review; report-writing.

A strong build with a weak pitch loses to a modest build with a strong one. Lead
with the problem the judge must feel; show the working thing rather than
describing it; claim only what is true, because a caught overclaim discredits
everything.
