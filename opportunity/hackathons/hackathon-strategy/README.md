# hackathon-strategy

Plans a hackathon entry to win within its real constraints: reads the judging
criteria and builds toward them, scopes a project to the time and team, plans the
build so a working demo exists early and improves, allocates the team, and
protects the demo and submission from the last-hour collapse.

- Inputs: the chosen event and its rules, the team, the time.
- Outputs: judging strategy, scoped project, build plan, team allocation.
- Depends on: opportunity-core.
- Downstream: pitch-and-demo, the engineering build skills.

Most hackathon projects lose not to better ideas but to worse scoping. Finish
something whole and small, then make it better, rather than build something big
and demo it broken. Read the rubric first; it decides everything.
