# Example: presenting a real profile at its strongest, without invention

A candidate transitioning from teaching to product management. The temptation is
to invent product experience. Career-core presents the real experience at its
strongest instead.

## What the candidate actually did

```
- Taught secondary maths for six years
- Ran the school's timetable software rollout: chose the tool, trained 40 staff,
  handled the migration, resolved adoption problems over a term
- Led a curriculum redesign coordinating eight teachers to a deadline
```

## The dishonest version (refused)

```
"Product Manager with experience shipping software to 40+ users and leading
cross-functional teams."
```

This invents a title the candidate never held and implies product-management
experience they do not have. It fails at the first interview question.

## The honest, strong version

```
"Educator moving into product management, with direct experience of the work
product managers do: I led my school's rollout of new timetabling software,
choosing the tool against our needs, training forty staff, and owning the
migration and the adoption problems that followed. I coordinated eight colleagues
through a curriculum redesign to a fixed deadline. I am now formalising this into
a product role."
```

Every claim is true and supportable. The rollout is framed in product language
(chose against needs, owned migration, drove adoption) because that is what it
genuinely was, not because the words were borrowed.

## The match analysis that goes with it

```
strong    stakeholder management, driving adoption, delivering to a deadline,
          learning a domain fast: all evidenced, all transferable
gap       no experience with a product analytics stack, a roadmap tool, or
          working with engineers; this is real and named
close it  a named plan: a product course, a side project shipping something small
          with a developer, to convert the gap into evidence
```

## The lesson

The honest version is not weaker; it is more persuasive, because it survives
scrutiny and the invented version does not. Career-core's job was to find the
true product-shaped experience already in the candidate's history and frame it
well, not to manufacture experience that was not there.
