# cv-engineering

Builds and improves a CV from the candidate's real profile: the format the target
market and tracking systems expect, achievement bullets that quantify a real
result, tailoring to a role without invention, ruthless relevance and length, and
a parsing pass for the machine that reads before the human does.

- Inputs: the career profile, the target role from job-search.
- Outputs: CV document, achievement bullets, tailoring notes, ATS check.
- Depends on: career-core, career-profile.
- Downstream: cover-letter, interview-preparation; document-design and pdf-production for rendering.

A CV has two readers: a machine that parses it in a second and a human who scans
it in six. It must survive both and be true. Tailoring reorders and reframes real
history; it never adds a skill or result the candidate does not have.
