# research-director

Research direction: identifying needs, depth levels, source hierarchy,
cross-verification, translation into narrative material, anachronism checks,
sensitive subjects, traceability.

- Inputs: master outline, genre, period, places.
- Outputs: research dossier, source sheets, verification notes.
- Depends on: `writing-constitution`.
- Downstream: `world-builder`, `immersion-director`,
  `character-psychologist`.

## When to use

Before writing about a real trade, period or place.

## The two rules that keep it useful

You research a scene, not a subject: research with no narrative need behind it
is an escape from writing. And step 7: under ten percent of what you found may
appear in the text, or the text is a lecture.

## Configuration

`language.creative_output` sets the output language.
