# horror

Horror writing: the three fears, seven-stage construction of the threat,
economy of showing, the rhythm of fear, character rationality, prohibited
cliches.

- Inputs: premise, place, rules of the threat.
- Outputs: terror plan, horror scenes.
- Depends on: `writing-constitution`, `scene-builder`, `immersion-director`.
- Neighbours: `dark-fantasy`, `thriller`.

## When to use

A horror story, a supernatural narrative, or a single scene of terror.

## The two rules that decide the result

Seventy percent terror, twenty horror, ten revulsion. Inverting that produces
disgust rather than fear. And characters must act rationally: a stupid
decision reassures the reader about their own safety, which is the one thing
horror cannot afford.

## Configuration

`language.creative_output` sets the output language.
