# political-fiction

Political fiction: the double-scale principle, the mechanics of power, level 3
research requirements, characters not reducible to their function, meeting
scenes as scenes of conflict.

- Inputs: institution, research dossier, the stake.
- Outputs: force map, outline.
- Depends on: `writing-constitution`, `research-director`,
  `character-psychologist`.
- Neighbours: `espionage`, `dystopian`, `historical-fiction`.

## When to use

Narratives of institutions, campaigns, or how a public decision gets made.

## The principle that carries it

Every scene of power is doubled by a scene of effect: a decision taken in a
meeting is paid for three chapters later by someone who knows nothing about
the meeting. Without that second scene the novel is a procedural with no
stake.

## Configuration

`language.creative_output` sets the output language.
