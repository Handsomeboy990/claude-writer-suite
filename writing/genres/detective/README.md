# detective

Investigation novel: choice of school, construction of the investigator, a
nine-step progression, professional truth, interrogation technique, prohibited
cliches.

- Inputs: clue table, research dossier on the milieu.
- Outputs: investigation dossier, procedure.
- Depends on: `writing-constitution`, `mystery`, `character-psychologist`.
- Neighbours: `thriller`, `political-fiction`.

## When to use

Crime fiction, a procedural, or a roman noir, where the investigator matters
as much as the solution.

## Boundary with mystery

`mystery` organises the puzzle and the fairness of the clues. This skill
organises the method, the milieu and the gaze. A procedural still needs
`mystery` underneath it for the clue table.

## Configuration

`language.creative_output` sets the output language.
