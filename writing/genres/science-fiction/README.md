# science-fiction

Rigorous science fiction: formulating the novum, the four-level consequence
method, requirements by subgenre, infrastructure and breakdown rules,
exposition without lecturing.

- Inputs: the starting hypothesis, research dossier.
- Outputs: a stated novum, technology bible, outline.
- Depends on: `writing-constitution`, `world-builder`, `research-director`.
- Neighbours: `cyberpunk`, `dystopian`.

## When to use

Hard SF, near-future fiction, space opera, post-apocalyptic, alternate
history.

## The method that carries the genre

One novum, four levels of consequence: direct, economic, social, intimate. The
fourth is the one most often skipped and the one that separates a novel from
an essay in costume.

## Configuration

`language.creative_output` sets the output language.
