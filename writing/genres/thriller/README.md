# thriller

Thriller writing: vital stake, visible deadline, competent antagonist, rising
cost. Tension mechanics, chapter rhythm, prohibited cliches, procedural
credibility.

- Inputs: premise, master outline, research dossier.
- Outputs: thriller outline, tension scenes.
- Depends on: `writing-constitution`, `novel-architect`, `scene-builder`.
- Neighbours: `espionage`, `mystery`.

## When to use

A countdown narrative, a race against time, or any story whose engine is
pressure rather than puzzle.

## The one resource

Time running out. Section 8 is the operational form: the deadline is recalled
every twenty pages by a fact rather than a sentence, and the threat is
partially realised at least once before the climax, or the reader does not
believe it.

## Configuration

`language.creative_output` sets the output language.
