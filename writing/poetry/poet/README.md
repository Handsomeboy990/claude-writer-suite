# poet

General poetry skill: French prosody (metres, caesura, rhyme, enjambment),
work on image and sound, a seven-step composition procedure, prohibitions,
raised quality thresholds.

- Inputs: a core, a constraint, a target form.
- Outputs: poems, prosody note.
- Depends on: `writing-constitution`.
- Downstream: `sonnet`, `haiku`, `free-verse`, `prose-poetry`.

## When to use

To compose or revise a poem, or to verify a syllable count.

## Output language

Section 2 is French prosody: mute e, dieresis, hemistich, masculine and
feminine rhyme. It applies to French output. Sections 3 to 7, image, sound,
procedure and prohibitions, apply in any language. Another output language
replaces section 2 with its own metrics, stated explicitly rather than
approximated from French.

## Threshold

Average at least 4, against 3.8 elsewhere in this tree. A publishable poem
demands more than publishable prose.
